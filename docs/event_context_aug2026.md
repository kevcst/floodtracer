# Event context: the August 2026 storm in eastern Uruguay

Press and SINAE/CECOED reports for the four areas in FloodTracer (northern Rocha, Treinta y Tres, Laguna Merín rice fields, Río Branco / Jaguarão). Compiled 14–15 Nov 2026. These are **preliminary figures used only as context** (team rule 4): they never enter the radar analysis. Verify with SINAE (gub.uy/sistema-nacional-emergencias) before publishing them as final.

## Why the water stayed (hypotheses the radar can check)

1. **Backwater from the Laguna Merín.** The lagoon received the heaviest rain in the country and the eastern rivers (Olimar, Cebollatí, Yaguarón) drain into it. With the lagoon high, those rivers drain more slowly and water stays longer in the fields. NISAR shows water in the fields on 16 Aug, a peak on 28 Aug and a slow retreat through September.
2. **Soils already wet.** July and early-August rains left low fields flooded before the storm; the radar sees this water and keeps it apart.
3. **The border does not stop the water.** The same flood reaches Río Branco (Uruguay) and Jaguarão (Brazil).
